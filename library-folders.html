03

Library Folders

HoneyBook · Information architecture · 2023

How do you bring order to a growing library of templates and files without adding friction to everyday use?

Role
Product designer
Team
PM · engineering · research
Timeline
2023
Scope
Research to ship

Context

The library was one unorganized, flat list. Users had to scroll through every image and file with no way to filter or group assets. There was no folder, tag, or category to lean on.

Finding one specific image became a time-consuming, frustrating process, and that friction compounded every time someone needed to reuse a template or asset.

Process

I designed a folder model that lets people group assets their own way, validated with a card sort so the structure matched their mental model rather than the system's. Alongside it, a right-click menu covers the everyday actions (rename, move, remove from a folder, set as a project header, delete) so reorganizing doesn't mean touching every item one at a time.

Finding

Outcome

Shipped a hierarchical folder system with bulk move and organize actions across the library, turning a flat scroll into a structure members shape themselves.
