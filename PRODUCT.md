# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Design leaders, hiring managers, and recruiters at product companies evaluating Avia Eyal for senior product design roles. They arrive from a CV link or LinkedIn, usually on a laptop between meetings, and decide within a minute or two whether to read a case study and reach out. A secondary audience is peers and collaborators who look her up after meeting her.

## Product Purpose

Present Avia's selected work and skills convincingly enough to earn a conversation. The site holds four HoneyBook case studies (Selective Communication, Custom Fields, Library Folders, Client Portal Redesign), an About page, interactive product mockups, and a CV. Success looks like a visitor opening a case study, reading it through, and then emailing or connecting on LinkedIn.

## Positioning

A designer for the complex parts of software — editors, file structure, platform features — who works from evidence (research, QA rigor, a neuroscience background) to shipped UI. The single claim every page reinforces: complexity, handled with evidence.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: email (aviaeyal@gmail.com). Secondary: LinkedIn, CV download (uploads/avia-eyal-cv-v10.pdf).
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: "She designs the complex parts, and proves her decisions."
- Belief ladder: (1) she handles gnarly B2B problems, not just surfaces → (2) her process is evidence-driven, research to shipped UI → (3) the outcomes were real, at a real company → (4) she would raise our team's bar → email her.
- Proof on hand: four case studies with real research numbers (146 votes, 30-member study, 80%+ task success, 444 votes), interactive product mockups (the *Mockup.dc.html files, rendered by support.js), assets/library-before.png, and the CV PDF.

## Brand Personality

Exacting, warm, evidence-led. First-person, plainspoken, specific — numbers sit next to the claims they support. The interface should evoke trust and quiet confidence, with a small spark of hand-drawn warmth (Avia also illustrates). Never salesy, never vague.

## Anti-references

- The 2026 minimal-cream portfolio template: warm near-white background, tiny mono uppercase eyebrow labels, hairline rules, whisper-gray text. The previous version of this site.
- Dribbble-style shot grids: pretty tiles with no argument.
- Editorial display-serif-italic magazine affectation.
- Dark "developer terminal" costume — she is a designer-scientist, not a terminal.

## Design Principles

1. Evidence first — every claim sits next to its number, quote, or artifact. The site argues like a case file.
2. The site is Exhibit A — its own hierarchy, information architecture, and craft demonstrate the skills it lists.
3. Complexity, made legible — dense material presented with calm structure, never simplified into vagueness.
4. A human hand on a rigorous grid — warmth comes from drawn annotation and voice, not from tinted backgrounds.
5. Fast to the point — a visitor decides in a minute, so the first fold of every page must carry the argument.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: body text ≥4.5:1, large text and graphics ≥3:1, visible focus states, full keyboard paths, and a reduced-motion alternative for every animation. Interactive mockups are presented as captioned figures so their content is described in text.
