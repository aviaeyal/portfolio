02

Custom Fields

HoneyBook · Data model · 2023

How do you let every business track the details unique to their work, without bloating the product for everyone else?

Role
Product designer
Team
PM · engineering · research
Timeline
2023
Scope
Scoping to ship

Context

No two service businesses track the same things. A photographer cares about shoot locations, a coach about session counts. The fixed fields forced everyone into notes and workarounds, and it showed up directly in what people were asking for:

Hi there, I'm curious if I'm able to add fields into a contact detail section. Is this possible?

I was able to create a new field within the project… but I'm only able to create text fields. Is it possible to create dropdowns or other types of fields?

The challenge wasn't adding fields. It was keeping the product simple for the people who would never need them.

Process

I ran discovery calls with two power users to pressure-test the model. Their expectations converged on the same handful of behaviors. Renaming a field should update it everywhere it's used, not break silently the way it did in tools they'd left behind. Most fields would live on the contact rather than the project. And quantity mattered less than reliability: one user's clients needed five fields or fewer 90% of the time, while the other wanted headroom up to 25.

  • 90%of contacts needed 5 fields or fewer
  • 20–25fields requested as headroom
  • 7competitor CRMs benchmarked

Pressure test

To check that five types were enough, I ran the model against a real job: a member who sends clients a birthday card and wants the right details ready when they do.

FieldTypeNotes
Cake flavorClosed liste.g. vanilla vs. chocolate
Contact groupText / multi-selectused to target the reminder
Lead sourceText / multi-selectvendor market vs. referral
Reference imageLinkkept hidden from the client

I also benchmarked field flexibility across seven competitor tools (Zoho, Spotler CRM, Bigin, Bloom, Copilot, Kartra, and Dubsado) to calibrate how far to push customization versus how simple to keep the defaults.

Finding

Outcome

Shipped a flexible custom-fields system that scales from a solo freelancer to a growing team without overwhelming either: five deliberate field types, rename-safe everywhere a field is used, and a 25-field cap that keeps the common case simple.

Status: shipped to beta. Adoption metrics were still collecting when this case file was written.
